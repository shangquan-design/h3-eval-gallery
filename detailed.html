B0 — Overview

This artifact organizes already-generated MiniMax-H3 evaluation videos into a 4-way comparison (Dense full-step baseline vs. Turbo8 vs. our DMD8 vs. OpenVDN's VDN-H3) and a Dense + DMD8 training-iteration progression (iter75–iter700), across the same 14 held-out prompts used throughout this project. All media are reused exactly as stored on disk — no fresh inference, training, or evaluation was run to build this artifact. Each row plays, pauses, restarts, and stays time-synchronized together (row controls, or any individual video's own controls); by default only Dense's audio is unmuted, with a per-row picker to switch which clip is heard.

Dense — stock MiniMax-H3, no LoRA, ~50-step full diffusion (the base/teacher arm; see B1)
Turbo8 — public LightX2V 8-step Turbo LoRA (bug-fixed this project; see h3_8step_threeway_compare/REPORT.md)
DMD8 — our own DMD-distilled 8-step student, main arm = iter700 (see B1)
VDN-H3 — OpenVDN's vdn-minimax-h3, 8-step, rung-0
DMD iteration milestone — a training checkpoint snapshot (iter75/150/300/500/700)

B4 — Focused findings / known phenomena

Everything in this block is an AI screening pass by this assistant (dense frame-grid inspection of the stored videos), NOT a human evaluation. Treat as a starting point for human review.
  • Turbo8 (public LightX2V 8-step LoRA, bug-fixed this project) and our DMD8 (iter700) are close on this board and both generally ahead of VDN-H3 (OpenVDN); VDN-H3's two consistent weaknesses across this whole project's history are membrane/rigid-fracture physics (water balloon never bursts, glass under-shatters) and volcano dynamism (stays comparatively static).
  • DMD8's clearest win: n_water_balloon_splash -- visible membrane rupture, present from as early as iter75, the strongest and most consistent physics win DMD8 has over both other arms.
  • DMD8's one clear, repeat weakness: s_incense_smoke_curl -- thin, underdeveloped smoke, and the only prompt in this review showing a visible degradation trend across DMD milestones (richest at iter75, thinnest by iter500-700).
  • DMD8's h_argument style-drift-to-anime defect (previously documented at iter500 in h3_8step_threeway_compare/REPORT.md) is confirmed here to be milestone-specific: present at iter300 and iter500, ABSENT at iter75, iter150, and iter700. This is why iter700 (not iter500) was chosen as the main 3-way comparison arm.
  • No general 'fire-focused training hurts unrelated prompt types' pattern was found: two clearly out-of-fire-domain prompts (n_glass_shatter_impact, s_building_demolition) stayed strong at every milestone from iter75 to iter700. The one prompt that did regress (incense smoke) is smoke-adjacent, not clearly explained by domain alone -- reported as an observed pattern, not a diagnosed cause.
  • A prior report (openvdn_compare/REPORT.md) claimed dmd_student_iter500 had a black final frame on ~8/14 prompts; a numeric last-frame check across all reviewed milestones here (read-only, no fresh inference) did not reproduce that on any milestone including iter500, and a fresh contact-sheet render of one flagged prompt no longer shows the black cell the previously-stored contact-sheet image shows -- most likely a one-off contact-sheet rendering artifact at generation time, not a real defect. Not corrected in the original report here (out of this task's scope), only flagged.
