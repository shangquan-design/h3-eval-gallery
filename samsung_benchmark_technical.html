A physics-focused comparison of Hedra's conservative optimized serving path, the previous Hedra serving baseline, and Fal H3 Max. The primary speed claim is end-to-end wall latency on the same 124-frame / 5.17-second output.

Executive summary

Precomputed AdaLN + original VAE overlap32 reduces matched-length Hedra wall latency from 3.72 s to 3.57 s while preserving quality relative to the previous serving path. Both Hedra variants are substantially faster end-to-end than the Fal API. Fal remains faster on reported model-only inference (2.66 s vs ~3.02 s), so this page makes no model-only speed claim for Hedra.

Methodology and reproducibility

Same original prompt text, 1344×768 Hedra canvas, native 4-step schedule, phase6_gate1300_runA checkpoint, warm persistent serving, and matched 124-frame / 5.17-second primary output. Fal used the same prompt, 768P 16:9 / 5 s setting, and prompt_expansion_mode="disabled". Hedra wall latency includes request completion and MP4 download; Fal wall and Fal-reported timings.inference are recorded separately. MI355X remains pending target-hardware validation.

Source of truth: page source, /data/home/sunsean/work/h3_speed/REPORT.md, raw result directories, and physics_expand/score_summary.json.
