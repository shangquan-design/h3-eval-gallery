Metric definition and setup

Verified score averages spatial IoU, weighted spatial IoU, spatiotemporal IoU, and inverted MSE after normalizing by physical variance and clipping to 0–1. 100 means the generated continuation is as close as a second real take; 0 means no overlap in where and when things move. Setup: image-to-video from the switch frame, 5 s at 768P / 24 fps; 8-step LoRA used h3_8step_preview.safetensors with USP 4 × TP 2, while H3 Max raw used Fal with prompt expansion disabled. Our 4-step follow-up is separately scored on raw prompts and should not be read as the 8-step result.
