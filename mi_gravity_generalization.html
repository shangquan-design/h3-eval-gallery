Gravity attribute vector: generalization check across 5 scenes

Negative result · neither locus produces a reliable gravity effect on any held-out scene

Follow-up to the hidden-state and velocity-space attribute-vector pages, both of which found zero visible gravity effect on a single held-out scene (paper-fall-water). Before concluding the gravity direction itself is the problem (not just that one scene), the already-built vectors -- no reconstruction, same .pt files -- were applied at full magnitude to 4 more held-out scenes, none used anywhere in building either vector.

Conclusion: across 5 held-out scenes now (this page's 4 plus the original paper-fall-water), neither locus produces a reliable "makes things fall better" effect. Hidden-state is a constant, content-independent artifact (brighter + blurrier, every single scene, same magnitude) -- not a gravity effect at all. Velocity is better-behaved (stays visually coherent) but inconsistent: no effect on 2/4 new scenes, a timing shift with a new defect (object disappearance) on one, and a trajectory change that looks like a worse outcome rather than a better one on another. This isn't one unlucky test scene -- gravity looks like a genuine weak point for this method, in either locus.
