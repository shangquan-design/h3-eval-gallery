Gravity attribute vector: generalization check, expanding to 20 scenes

In progress · scale reduced to 0.25x after feedback · 4 of 20 scenes shown so far, 16 more running

Follow-up to the hidden-state and velocity-space attribute-vector pages, both of which found zero visible gravity effect on a single held-out scene (paper-fall-water) at full magnitude. The first pass here (4 scenes, also at full magnitude) confirmed that: hidden-state showed a constant yellow/blur artifact, and velocity showed an unnaturally strong overshoot on one scene (ball-and-block-fall: object bounced back up into the grabber tool). Scaling both vectors down to 0.25x of their original magnitude removes most of that distortion -- the numbers and clips below are the 0.25x versions. A batch of 16 more held-out scenes is running now at the same 0.25x scale to build out a larger, more representative set.

Reading so far: at 0.25x, both loci stay visually close to baseline -- the artifact from full magnitude is mostly gone. What hasn't appeared is a confirmed, repeatable "makes things fall better/faster" effect: no scene here shows a clean, unambiguous improvement over baseline physics. The closer-to-natural ball-and-block-fall result at 0.25x is an improvement in coherence, not confirmed evidence of a stronger gravity pull. Treat this page as provisional until the 16-scene batch lands.
