Does more weight change show up on screen?

DMPDD (ref2va, MiniMax-H3 base) at checkpoint-2000 with LoRA rank 128 and LR 1e-4, compared with the untrained original on 30 held-out ref2va prompts. A fourth column adds the confirmed gate-fix retrain (v2, see footnote).

Reading the grid: reference image, then original, then the rank128/LR1e-4 checkpoint, then the same recipe retrained with a gate-specific, 100x-smaller learning rate.
Why this run: earlier runs at rank 64 / LR 2e-5 barely moved the weights. Raising rank and LR to the production recipe gives a much larger change, and it stays stable: no collapse or broken anatomy across all 30 prompts.

Gate-fix column, v2 (confirmed working): rank128/LR1e-4 had a subtle mid-clip darkening/flicker (most visible around 1s and 4s in some clips), traced to to_gate_compress (VSA's compression-compensation gate) being excluded from the LoRA's target modules. A first attempt (v1) added the gate to the LoRA at the same rank/LR as everything else -- a direct weight-diff showed it drifting 68-178% relative to its base value (two orders of magnitude more than attn.to_q), and it actually made the darkening worse by the final checkpoint, even though an early checkpoint had looked clean (just not enough total training yet to show it). v2 gives the gate its own learning rate, 100x smaller (1e-6) than the main 1e-4, via a small addition to the training framework (per-parameter-group learning rates). Verified on the full 2000-step run: 15/16 previously-affected prompts show no brightness dip at all; the one remaining case (train speeding) is a normal motion-blur frame, not the darkening artifact.
