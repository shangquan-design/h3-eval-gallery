Why include these notes?

They are useful counter-examples and keep the review balanced; aggregate metrics do not replace visual judgment.

Method

Model. Hedra's physics DMD LoRA on MiniMax-H3, bf16, LoRA merged into the weights. Native 4-step schedule (sigmas 1, 0.923, 0.75, 0), no classifier-free guidance, no prompt rewriting.

Serving. One persistent server per configuration, all models resident on GPU, Ulysses-8 sequence parallelism. The first request after startup is treated as warmup; all reported requests are warm.

Prompts. 10 water and fluid-physics prompts with fixed seeds, identical text and seeds for Hedra and Fal. Fal called with prompt_expansion_mode=disabled, 768P, 16:9, duration 5.

Output. MP4, H.264 video with AAC stereo audio at 32 kHz. Hedra encodes with x264 veryfast at CRF 21.

Model inference. Hedra: server-side time from request start to decoded frames, before MP4 encoding. Fal: timings.inference as returned by the API; its exact scope is not documented.

MI355. Same checkpoint, schedule, prompts and settings; the run package is ready. Results will replace the pending row.

Evidence & target hardware

Canonical evidence. The benchmark report, raw latency results, score aggregates, and checksums are retained in the internal benchmark workspace. They are not mirrored to this public gallery.

Video provenance. This page uses the verified Hedra 8×H100 and Fal H3 Max originals selected for the four-row showcase; no new inference was run for publication.
