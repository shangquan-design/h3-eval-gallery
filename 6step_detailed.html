D0 — Overview

This artifact compares our current best checkpoints across two step counts (6-step vs. our previous best 8-step) side by side with two external 8-step references (LightX2V's public Turbo8 LoRA and OpenVDN's VDN-H3), across the same 14 held-out prompts used throughout this project (h3_sft/prompts/holdout.json). All media are reused exactly as stored on disk — no fresh inference, training, or evaluation was run to build this artifact. Each row plays, pauses, restarts, and stays time-synchronized together (row controls, or any individual video's own controls); every video starts muted (the row's "Mute all" picker option), with a per-row picker to choose exactly one video to unmute.

No new quality assessment was performed to build this gallery page. The tie/win/loss judgments in D2/D4 below are reported verbatim from the historical AI screening pass documented in h3_sft_dmd/PHASE4_GATE350_FULL14_AND_BENCHMARK.md (that report states its own comparisons are a full-video-review AI screening pass, explicitly not a human evaluation) — and cover only our 6-step vs. our previous best 8-step. Turbo8 and VDN-H3 are shown as reference columns for side-by-side viewing only; no tie/win/loss claim is made against them here or in that report.
Our 6-step — phase4_c_gate350.safetensors
Our previous best 8-step — phase3_c_gate250.safetensors (not iter700/iter1000)
Turbo8 — LightX2V public 8-step Turbo LoRA (reference column, bug-fixed scale per h3_8step_threeway_compare/REPORT.md)
VDN-H3 — OpenVDN vdn-minimax-h3, 8-step (reference column)

D1 — Model descriptions

Our 6-step (phase4_c_gate350.safetensors) — our DMD-distilled student, Phase 4 of the campaign, trained from the Phase 3 gate250 checkpoint (student+fake+optimizer state carried over via auto_resume) for +100 iterations with num_inference_steps dropped from 8 to 6 (the only changed variable vs. Phase 3), same hard-case-weighted training data, video_flow_shift unchanged at 6.0. Passed all three required gates (+25/+50/+100 iterations) on the reduced hard-case board, then validated on the full 14-prompt holdout set. See h3_sft_dmd/PHASE4_6STEP_RESULT.md and h3_sft_dmd/PHASE4_GATE350_FULL14_AND_BENCHMARK.md.

Our previous best 8-step (phase3_c_gate250.safetensors) — the Phase 3 checkpoint this project's own DMD campaign considered its best 8-step Pareto point before Phase 4. This is not the same lineage as the dmd_student_iter700/iter1000 checkpoints used as the "DMD8" arm in simple.html/detailed.html — those are milestone snapshots from a different (earlier, non-gated) training run. phase3_c_gate250 is the specific checkpoint the 6-step result in D2 below is benchmarked against.

LightX2V Turbo8 (minimax_h3_fl2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_768p_bf16.safetensors, HF lightx2v/Minimax-h3-Turbo) — a public 8-step distillation LoRA, not affiliated with this project's DMD campaign. Shown here as a reference column only; reused from the already-published threeway-comparison assets (h3_8step_threeway_compare/results/), the LoRA-scale-bug-fixed set (see that project's own REPORT.md §1) — not the corrupted h3_sft_dmd/outputs/eval_full/*_turbo8.mp4 set.

VDN-H3 8-step (OpenVDN vdn-minimax-h3, checkpoint stage-dmd-step-250) — a re-architected model (frozen MiniMax-H3 backbone + trained linear-attention branch + LoRA adapters), not affiliated with this project's DMD campaign. Shown here as a reference column only; reused from the already-published threeway-comparison assets.

D2 — Result summary

This gallery build performed no new quality assessment. The tie/win/loss result below is reported verbatim from the historical AI screening pass (full-video review, not a human evaluation) documented in the source report at the time it was written.

14/14 ties, 0 wins, 0 losses -- 6-step (gate350) vs previous best 8-step (gate250). No new failure modes were found on any of the 14 prompts. Specifically preserved relative to the previous best 8-step:

  • water-balloon rupture (n_water_balloon_splash)
  • campfire/background richness (s_campfire_sparks)
  • train-window moderate camera-motion/parallax (h_train_window)
  • clean h_argument (no anime/style drift)

Source report: h3_sft_dmd/PHASE4_GATE350_FULL14_AND_BENCHMARK.md. Ties are only claimed between our 6-step and our previous best 8-step. Turbo8 and VDN-H3 are reference columns shown for side-by-side viewing only -- no tie/win/loss claim is made against them in this artifact.

D3 — Speed / VRAM (with caveats)

same GPU (1x 80GB H100-class card), same harness (dmd_student_eval.py's make_pipe/generate, enable_auto_cpu_offload NOT disabled), sequential same-node run. single measurement per arm, not averaged (this offload harness sits at its ~70GB VRAM ceiling; repeated calls in-process OOM'd).

MetricOur 6-step (gate350)Our previous best 8-step (gate250)
E2E latency138.90s147.11s
Denoising steps68
Peak VRAM70.29GB70.29GB

Correct framing: 8.21s / 5.6% lower E2E latency for the 6-step arm in this harness. Separately, the 6-step arm requests 25% fewer denoising steps (NOT the same as 25% faster E2E -- this harness is offload-bound, not denoise-bound; see caveat) — a real reduction in compute, but this was not isolated and measured as a standalone per-step speedup in this task; treat it as a step-count fact, not a measured timing claim. Peak VRAM is identical between the two arms (the CPU-offload harness pins VRAM near its ceiling regardless of step count).

This is a same-GPU, same-harness research comparison, one measurement per arm (not averaged), CPU-offload bound -- not a denoise-only or production-serving benchmark, and not a promise of deployment-grade gains. The 25% step-count reduction is a real reduction in requested denoising steps but was not separately measured as an isolated per-step or fixed-overhead speedup -- no such breakdown is reported here. No speed numbers are reported here for Turbo8/VDN-H3 -- they were not re-measured in this same harness for this task and are omitted rather than presented out of context.
